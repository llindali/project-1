## Reported versus organic growth

| Year | Reported growth, filing rounded | Volume contribution | Price contribution | FX contribution | Organic/same-store measure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| [2023][2023] | 20% | 16% | 4% | 0% | No separate comparable organic or same-store series identified |
| [2024][2024] | 32% | 27% | 5% | 0% | No separate comparable organic or same-store series identified |
| [2025][2025] | 45% | 50% | -6% | 1% | No separate comparable organic or same-store series identified |

Organic growth means growth from the comparable existing business, excluding acquisitions/divestitures and often currency effects, according to the issuer's definition. Lilly reports a volume/price/FX bridge. Volume is **not automatically organic growth**: FY2023 included product-rights sales, and FY2024 included a collaboration payment. A rough volume-plus-price result is not a verified organic measure. The model therefore labels its growth path guidance or judgment, not historical organic growth.

ABG's 1.8% is a forecast judgment anchored to existing-store growth; 4.7% reported growth includes acquired dealerships. Compounding acquisition-related sales without paying for those acquisitions would overstate cash generation. Linda must explain this to her partner in her own words.
